1. Executive Summary
This document is the master technical and design specification for the Andromeda Logic Corp corporate website. It translates four benchmark reference sites into a concrete, page-by-page build plan — information architecture, visual system, module/sub-module/feature breakdown, and an AI assistant specification — so that design and engineering can move directly into production.
1.1 Purpose of This Document
Andromeda Logic Corp requires a flagship corporate website capable of communicating deep-technical credibility to space agencies, commercial operators, defense programs, and research institutions simultaneously. Rather than starting from a blank page, this specification is built from a structured teardown of four sites that already solve adjacent problems well — a global AI/engineering consultancy, its training-academy sub-brand, a compliance-first software house, and a private orbital-launch company. Section 5 documents exactly what was observed on each site and why it is relevant; every later section shows where that observation was adopted, adapted, or deliberately rejected for Andromeda Logic Corp’s context.
The document is written to be handed directly to a cross-functional delivery team. Product and design read Sections 1–8 for strategy, IA, and visual system; engineering reads Section 9 module-by-module as build tickets; the AI/ML team reads Section 10 as the assistant’s functional spec; and platform/security reads Sections 11–14 for stack, compliance, and hardening requirements.
1.2 Project Overview
• Deliverable: a public, multi-page marketing and technical-credibility website (not a customer portal or mission-ops console) for Andromeda Logic Corp.
• Primary goal: convert space agencies, commercial operators, defense programs, and research institutions into qualified mission-consultation leads and technology-licensing conversations.
• Secondary goal: establish Andromeda Logic Corp as a citable technical authority in autonomous GNC, radiation-tolerant AI, edge computing, and HITL simulation — supporting recruiting, press, and investor relations.
• Differentiator: a brand-owned AI assistant (“NOVA”, Section 10) surfaced site-wide as a flagship capability, not a support-desk widget.
• Target scale: ≈14 primary page templates, a component-driven design system, and a phased 3-release roadmap (Section 16).

Andromeda Logic Corp is a space and research technology company building autonomous, radiation-tolerant computing systems for deep-space missions. The summary below is the source-of-truth brand content supplied for this project; it is condensed here strictly as the reference the design and copy teams should build every page against. 3. Target Audience & User Personas
Four personas drive every navigation, content, and CTA decision in this document. Each persona reappears as a dedicated entry card in the Solutions-by-Audience module (Section 9.4) and shapes the tone, proof points, and conversion path offered on that path.
3.1 Space Agencies
National and multinational agencies (e.g., NASA, ESA, ISRO-adjacent programs) evaluating autonomous GNC, radiation-tolerant compute, or HITL simulation partners for lunar, Mars, or deep-space programs. They need rigorous technical documentation, flight-heritage evidence, and a procurement-friendly RFP/consultation path.
3.2 Commercial Space Operators
Satellite constellation operators, private launch providers, and in-orbit servicing companies seeking licensed autonomy software, swarm orchestration, or edge-compute payloads. They respond to product spec sheets, integration documentation, and clear engagement/licensing models.
3.3 Defense & National Security Programs
Defense-adjacent programs requiring resilient, autonomous systems for contested or communication-denied environments. This audience is highly sensitive to security posture, export-control clarity, and data handling — driving the Trust, Compliance & Export Control Center (Section 9.14) and the site-wide consent posture (Section 8.3).
3.4 Research Institutions & Academia DEFENSE-ADJACENT POSTURE Because a meaningful share of Andromeda Logic Corp’s audience sits in the defense and national-security space, the entire public website is designed with a defense-adjacent security and data-handling posture by default — not layered on later. This shapes the first-visit consent gate (8.3), the export-control gating rule applied to gated downloads (9.14), and the conservative approach to what is ever published about active missions or program partners.
Universities, national laboratories, and independent research institutions seeking HITL simulation access, joint research programs, or citable technical publications. They engage primarily through the Technical Library (Section 9.10) and Research & Innovation page (Section 9.5), and convert through research-partnership inquiries rather than commercial sales paths.

7. Visual Design System
7.1 Design Philosophy: "Structured Cosmos"
Skyroot’s reference shows full-bleed cinematic imagery carrying the emotional weight while typography stays minimal and secondary (5.4). Andromeda Logic Corp pushes this further with a genuinely distinctive mechanism rather than a generic "dark tech" theme: every surface is treated as a instrument panel laid over deep space — a near-black canvas, thin luminous rule-lines standing in for orbital paths and telemetry grids, and a single signature multi-stop gradient ("Andromeda Drift") reserved exclusively for primary CTAs, section dividers, and the NOVA assistant — so the gradient always signals "this is an important, alive, AI-touched moment" rather than being decorative wallpaper.
• Dark-canvas-first: primary marketing surfaces (Home, Technology, Products) default to the Deep Space palette; documentation-dense surfaces (Technical Library, Trust Center) flip to a light canvas for long-form readability — mirroring how mission-critical dashboards use dark mode while reference manuals stay light.
• Telemetry-grid motif: thin 1px gradient hairlines and dot-grid backgrounds evoke orbital paths / sensor grids without becoming literal illustration.
• Gradient as signal, not skin: the Andromeda Drift gradient is rationed — primary CTA, active nav state, NOVA panel, and status-critical highlights only.
• Real imagery over iconography: per Skyroot’s precedent (5.4), genuine engineering/mission photography and clean 3D hardware renders are used wherever possible; icon systems are reserved for UI affordances, not hero storytelling.

10. NOVA — AI Mission Assistant (Recommended AI Chatbot Integration)
Both Xebia and Yalantis independently productize an AI assistant as a first-class brand capability rather than a support-desk bolt-on (5.1, 5.3). This is a strong enough signal, on top of Andromeda Logic Corp’s own AI/ML positioning, to make a named, brand-owned assistant a core recommendation of this specification — not an optional add-on. NOVA is specified in full here so AI/ML engineering can scope it as a first-phase deliverable.
10.1 Strategic Rationale
• Category credibility: a company that sells autonomous, intelligent systems for deep space should visibly demonstrate applied AI on its own front door. A generic third-party chat widget undercuts that story; a named, purpose-built assistant reinforces it.
• Navigation relief: the taxonomy is deep (14 primary templates, 5 specialties, 5 platforms, 4 personas). NOVA gives visitors — especially time-constrained procurement evaluators — a faster path than manual mega-menu traversal.
• Qualification at scale: NOVA can pre-qualify Mission Consultation inquiries (mission phase, persona, technology interest) before a human ever engages, per 10.3.

10.5 Technical Architecture LAYER RECOMMENDATION LLM Backend A hosted, enterprise-grade foundation model accessed via API, selected for strong retrieval-grounded answer quality and citation support rather than raw creative capability. Grounding / RAG Retrieval-augmented generation over a curated index of Technology, Products, Case Studies, and Technical Library content only — NOVA does not answer from unconstrained general knowledge on company-specific claims. 10.8 Success Metrics for NOVA
• Query-to-citation rate: share of answered questions that include a valid source citation (target: near-total for Knowledge Mode).
• Persona-routing accuracy: share of Solutions Navigator sessions that land the visitor on the correct persona path without manual correction.
• Pre-qualification completion rate: share of started Mission Consultation Pre-Qualifier flows that end in a submitted or edited form.
• Escalation-avoidance vs. escalation-appropriateness balance: NOVA should reduce unnecessary sales contact for simple technical questions while never blocking a visitor who wants a human — tracked via the "Talk to a human" exit path usage.
